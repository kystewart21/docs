# Source: https://data.gov/timeline

## January 21, 2009

Presidential [Memorandum](https://www.archives.gov/files/cui/documents/2009-WH-memo-on-transparency-and-open-government.pdf) on Transparency and Open Government.

2009

## May 21, 2009

GSA launches [Data.gov](https://web.archive.org/web/20090530020348/http://www.data.gov/)

## December 8, 2009

Open Government [Directive](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/open/documents/open-government-directive) requiring agencies to post high-value datasets on Data.gov

## June 11, 2012

U.S. and India launch [Open Government Platform](https://fedscoop.com/u-s-india-officially-launch-data-gov-in-a-box/) (open source package for government open data sites)

2012

## May 9, 2013

Open Data Executive [Order](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/the-press-office/2013/05/09/executive-order-making-open-and-machine-readable-new-default-government-)

2013

## May 9, 2013

Open Data [Policy](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/sites/default/files/omb/memoranda/2013/m-13-13.pdf)

## 2014

Data.gov team begins working in the open on [GitHub](https://github.com/GSA/data.gov)

2014

## 2015

Launch of [Project Open Data](https://github.com/project-open-data/project-open-data.github.io) on GitHub

2015

## December 10, 2016

Senate passes [OPEN Government Data Act](https://www.congress.gov/bill/114th-congress/senate-bill/2852?q=%7B%22search%22%3A%5B%22OPEN+Government+Data%22%5D%7D&s=6&r=1)

2016

## December 21, 2018

OPEN Government Data Act, incorporated into Foundations for Evidence-Based Policymaking [Act](https://www.congress.gov/bill/115th-congress/house-bill/4174/actions?s=4&r=1&q=%7B%22search%22%3A%5B%22Foundations+Evidence+Based%22%5D%7D) passes both Houses of Congress

2018

## January 14, 2019

OPEN Government Data Act signed into [law](https://www.congress.gov/115/plaws/publ435/PLAW-115publ435.pdf) (Data.gov required by statute)

2019

## July 2019

Launch of [resources.data.gov](https://resources.data.gov/)

## October 2020

Improved inventory.data.gov launched (metadata management tool)

2020

## February 2021

Data.gov catalog running updated [CKAN](https://ckan.org/)

2021

## August 2022

Completion of migration of Data.gov to [Cloud.gov](https://cloud.gov/) infrastructure

2022

## March 30, 2023

Launch of redesigned Data.gov [home](https://data.gov/) page

2023

## April 2026

Launch of redesigned [catalog.data.gov](https://catalog.data.gov/)

2026
