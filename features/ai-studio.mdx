The AI Studio is where you write, generate, and refine posts before they hit the calendar. Choose from seven AI models across Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and xAI; let voice learning mirror your tone automatically; and generate images or short video to attach — all inside a single editor, with nothing publishing until you approve it.

## Generating Posts in the AI Studio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI Studio">
    Go to **Dashboard → AI Studio**, or open the composer and click **AI Generate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your topic and tone">
    Enter a topic, choose a tone (casual, professional, punchy, etc.), and select one of the seven available AI models.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the variations">
    The Studio generates multiple tweet variations at once. Read through them and pick the one that fits best, or mix and match lines between variations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit, approve, and schedule">
    Make any final edits in the inline editor, then schedule the post to the calendar or publish immediately. Nothing leaves the Studio until you explicitly approve it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing an AI Model

The Studio gives you access to seven models from four providers. Different models suit different content styles — some are sharper for technical writing, others produce more natural conversational copy. Switch models freely between generations; your voice learning data carries over to each one.

## Voice Learning

After you publish 10 or more posts, OpenTweet enables Voice Learning automatically. The model analyses your publishing history — sentence length, vocabulary, punctuation habits, tone — and mirrors that style in every generation. You don't configure anything; it updates continuously as you publish more.

Switch AI models at any time. Voice learning adapts independently for each model so the output stays consistent no matter which one you pick.

## Generating Images and Video

The AI Studio isn't limited to text. From the composer or Studio editor:

- **Generate an image** — Enter a prompt and the Studio produces a ready-to-attach image for your post.
- **Generate a short video clip** — Describe the scene or motion you want and the Studio produces a short video you can attach directly.

Both options are available in the Studio and via the MCP tools described below.

## AI Tools in the MCP and REST API

Your AI agent has direct access to the Studio's capabilities through the MCP:

| MCP Tool | What it does |
|----------|-------------|
| `opentweet_repurpose_tweet` | Rewrite an existing post into new variations (different angle, tone, or format) |
| `opentweet_generate_image` | Generate an image from a text prompt for use in a post |
| `opentweet_generate_video` | Generate a short video clip from a text prompt |
| `opentweet_search_inspiration` | Search for reference tweets on a topic to inform drafting |
| `opentweet_create_tweet` | Create and schedule a tweet directly from the agent |
| `opentweet_get_content_gaps` | Identify topics you haven't posted about recently |

<Tip>
  Ask your AI agent to call `opentweet_get_content_gaps` to surface topics you haven't covered recently. Then have it generate a post on one of those topics using `opentweet_create_tweet` — you'll fill gaps in your content mix without having to audit your own timeline manually.
</Tip>
