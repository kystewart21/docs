Use `compare_schools` with required `ids`, an array of two to five integer federal UNITIDs. Optional `fields` selects upstream fields or supported aliases. Omit it for the worker's default field set.

The worker returns `source`, `retrieved_at` and `schools`. Comparison rows retain upstream field names, unlike the normalised single-school response. Do not assume those shapes are identical or that every requested institution returned a row.

Compare equivalent measures with their available reporting context. Average net price is not a personal aid quote, and costs should not be combined across different reporting periods without disclosure.

See [Authentication](/authentication) for required access and [Quickstart](/quickstart) for connection verification.
