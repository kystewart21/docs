BOX College Scorecard supports institutional research, subject to the service's access policy and the evidence returned by each request.

## Do I need to paste a government API key?

No. Do not put upstream credentials into chat or tool arguments. The service's server-side credential is separate from your own authorised connection. See [Authentication](/authentication).

## Does sharing the MCP connect the website?

No. Account access, the consuming Site's workspace permission and visitor consent are separate checks.

## Can I search programmes by a plain-language major?

The current `find_programs` schema requires `cip_code`. Translate the intended major carefully into the appropriate CIP, then check credential level and the exact institution. Do not send a keyword in place of a required CIP code.

## What does a fresh retrieval date mean?

It records when the service fetched the response. It does not establish the reporting year of each measure. The current worker queries `latest` fields. Where reporting years are not returned, show them as unknown.

## Can the result establish a baseball opportunity?

No. NCAA membership, baseball sponsorship, rosters, incoming commitments, coach-confirmed needs and individual financial-aid offers require independent evidence.

## How should missing values be handled?

Keep missing or suppressed values unknown. Do not substitute zero. Check the institution's identity using its returned federal UNITID, name and state before comparing it with another school.
