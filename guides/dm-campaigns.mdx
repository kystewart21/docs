DM Campaigns let you reach people who have already engaged with your posts — replied, liked, or followed — with a personalized direct message. Unlike fully automated DM tools, OpenTweet requires your explicit approval before any message is sent. Every draft sits in a review queue until you approve or skip it, and every approved message goes out through the official X DM API — no browser automation, no scraping, no account risk. DM Campaigns are available on Advanced and Agency plans.

## How Campaigns Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the campaign">
    Choose a **source** (replies to a specific post, likes on a post, or new followers) and write a **message template**. Use `{{name}}` and `{{context}}` placeholders where you want the AI to personalize each message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lead discovery">
    OpenTweet scans real engagement data from your chosen source and builds a list of candidate leads. Qualification filters (Advanced and Agency only) remove bots, private accounts, and profiles that don't match your criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Message drafting">
    The AI drafts a personalized message for each qualified lead using your template as a guide. Drafts are queued for your review — nothing is sent at this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your approval">
    Open the campaign inbox and review each draft. Click **Approve** to queue the message for delivery, or **Skip** to discard it. Nothing sends until you approve it individually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery">
    Approved messages are delivered through the official X DM API in the order you approved them. OpenTweet respects X's DM rate limits automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply tracking">
    Replies from leads flow back into the campaign inbox, grouped by conversation. Respond directly from the inbox or hand off to your agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a Campaign via MCP

If your agent is connected to the OpenTweet MCP server, create a campaign with a single natural-language prompt:

> "Create a DM campaign targeting people who replied to my MCP thread, with a message about [your offer]."

The agent calls `dm_campaign_create` and returns a campaign ID. You can then review and approve leads from the dashboard or via MCP.

---

## Approve Leads

You have two ways to approve queued messages:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Via MCP" icon="terminal">
    Call `dm_lead_approve` with the lead ID returned by the campaign. Useful for bulk-reviewing from a script or agent workflow — but still one approval per lead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Via Dashboard" icon="browser">
    Open the campaign, switch to the **Queued** tab, read each draft, and click **Approve** or **Skip**. The dashboard shows the lead's profile alongside their draft for context.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## View Campaign Results

Track the full funnel for any campaign using two MCP tools or the dashboard's analytics tab:

| Tool | What it returns |
|------|-----------------|
| `dm_campaign_report` | Full funnel: found → qualified → approved → sent → replied |
| `dm_inbox_list` | Reply conversations grouped by lead |

A typical campaign funnel looks like this:

```
Found: 412
Qualified: 96
Approved by you: 24
Sent: 24
Replied: 9
```

The gap between **Found** and **Qualified** reflects the qualification filters removing low-quality leads. The gap between **Qualified** and **Approved** is your own review — that friction is intentional and is what keeps campaigns effective and compliant.

---

<Warning>
  DM campaigns use the official X API to send messages. X enforces DM rate limits; OpenTweet respects them automatically. Never approve messages in bulk without reading them — personalization is what makes campaigns effective and what keeps them out of spam filters.
</Warning>

<Note>
  DM Campaigns require the **Advanced** ($29/mo) or **Agency** ($49/mo) plan.
</Note>
