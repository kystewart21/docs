title: "Research a reported major"
sidebarTitle: "Programme research"
description: "Use the current BOX College Scorecard worker and its verified tool schemas. Preserve institution identity, source metadata and explicit research limits."
---

Translate the student's actual intended major into a suitable CIP before calling BOX Scorecard `find_programs`. Specify `credential_level` when the qualification matters, and limit to an exact `school_id` after resolving identity.

Check the returned programme rows rather than assuming a successful query establishes the desired degree. Broad engineering interest does not identify a particular engineering branch. Confirm the current degree title, campus, entry requirements and course preparation with official institutional sources.

Retain uncertainty where the query fails or coverage is incomplete. Do not substitute a different major without the family's knowledge.

See [Authentication](/authentication) for required access and [Quickstart](/quickstart) for connection verification.
