---

Resolve every institution's UNITID and state before comparing schools with BOX College Scorecard. Retrieve consistent measures with `compare_schools` and retain the source and retrieval date.

Check academic programme fit, reported cost, admission context and geographic preference independently. An institution-level admission rate is not an athlete's admission probability. Average net price is a historical population measure, not the family's financial-aid offer.

Use official programme evidence for baseball division, staff, rosters, commitments and entry-year competition. Keep a research hold where evidence is missing. Never rank a school as a final target solely because one academic or athletic number is strong.
